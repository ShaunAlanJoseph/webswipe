WEBSWIPE TEAM RECORDING - POSES + WORDS (ABOUT 10 MINUTES)
Please send your ZIP to Shaun by Saturday evening, 10 October.
Allow extra time for first-time installation and reading these instructions.

1. SET UP AND LAUNCH
Extract this entire ZIP into a NEW folder. Do not run inside a ZIP preview.
You need a webcam, Python, and internet for dependency installation. Python 3.11
or 3.12 is a suitable choice; use python.org if you need to install it. On Windows,
enable "Add Python to PATH". The hand-tracking model is included; no GPU needed.
Windows: double-click record_windows.bat.
Mac/Linux: open Terminal in the extracted folder and run:
  bash record_mac_linux.sh
Enter your name/nickname, setting (e.g. roomday), camera number (usually 0), and
brief setup description. Use the same name if you record again.
Close other camera apps and allow camera access. These recordings include your
face/background; choose a setting you are comfortable sharing with the team.
Use normal typing distance and usable lighting. Keep your whole hand visible.
One setup is enough: do not spend your ten minutes changing cameras or rooms.

2. HAND POSES - 3 MINUTES 20 SECONDS
Four poses repeated twice. Each has 5 seconds GET READY, then 20 seconds REC.
Adopt the requested pose during GET READY and keep it throughout REC:
- RIGHT PINCH: thumb/index fingertips TOUCHING. Move across the keyboard as
  though swiping words. Other fingers can relax.
- RIGHT NEAR: thumb/index close but visibly NOT TOUCHING. Make similar movements.
- LEFT PINCH: thumb/index fingertips TOUCHING. Gently vary position and angle.
- LEFT NEAR: thumb/index close but visibly NOT TOUCHING. Gently vary position/angle.
Keep the other hand down. RIGHT/LEFT means your physical hand. Your left hand
can remain comfortably on the left; it need not reach the displayed keyboard.
Do not switch between touching and separated during one segment. NEAR does
not mean fully spread fingers or a fist. Gently vary angle without changing pose.
Click the camera window first. R = redo CURRENT segment; Q = stop; N = skip.
Q/N may leave poses incomplete. If a mistake advances before you press R, tell
Shaun which pose/round was affected when sending the ZIP.

3. WORD SWIPES - UP TO 6 MINUTES 40 SECONDS
When the pose window closes, return to the terminal and press Enter for words.
There are TWO practice words, then up to 30 word prompts. Practice and retries
count toward the timer. It starts when you press S on the first practice word.
Finish the list or let the timer stop it; fewer good words are better than rushing.

For EACH word:
  a. Use your RIGHT hand. Pinch thumb and index together and move the orange
     cursor to the FIRST letter. Press S to arm the recording.
  b. Hold the physical SPACE key with your LEFT hand. While holding it, move
     the pinched right hand through the word's letters in order. Do not tap
     each letter or release between letters. For repeated letters (hello),
     pause briefly on that letter without opening your pinch.
  c. At the LAST letter, RELEASE SPACE immediately while staying over the key.
     Then open your fingers. The recorder saves a short release-video tail.
  d. Inspect the drawn path: A = accept; R = retry your mistake; F = retry a
     tracking failure; N = skip a difficult word. Then press S for the next word.
The right-hand cursor must be visible before you begin. Keep your left hand
near the physical keyboard and out of the camera view while holding Space.
The displayed keyboard matches the app layout. Keep fingers pinched throughout
one word. SPACE controls recording boundaries even if pinch detection would fail.
No predicted word is shown: accept based on whether you followed the target and
whether the path tracked your movement. If you fumble Space, use R to retry.
If the window loses focus, that attempt is saved as interrupted; press S to retry.
Q = save and exit early. Retried/skipped attempts remain available for analysis.
Stop when the recorder finishes; no extra spelling or second pose session needed.

4. SEND ONE FILE
Return to the terminal and WAIT for "SEND THIS ONE FILE TO SHAUN".
The combined ZIP is in SEND_THESE_FILES/. It contains BOTH pose images and word
videos/paths. Upload that ZIP to your usual shared Drive/cloud folder and send
Shaun a link he can open. Do not send the kit, .venv, or screenshots instead.
A ZIP marked PARTIAL is still useful: send it and mention missing work/errors.
Mention recording mistakes. Nothing uploads automatically. Keep originals until
Shaun confirms receipt. Video may make the returned ZIP several hundred MB.

TROUBLESHOOTING
Wrong/black camera: close other camera apps, allow camera access, try camera 1.
Mac: allow camera access for Terminal/Python. Linux: use a desktop session, not WSL
or a headless server; install python3-venv or libGL if an error names either.
Installation failed: check internet and rerun the launcher. If there is no package
for your Python version, use Python 3.11/3.12 in a newly extracted kit folder.
Keys not working: click the recorder window, then use the displayed keys.
RIGHT hand not detected: bring it fully into view and rotate slightly so fingertips
are visible. If it never works, send Shaun the error/details; don't spend hours.
If the word tool fails, the wrapper still packages any saved poses and words.

WHAT IS SAVED
Poses: original camera images and pose labels implied by the recording segments.
Words: raw mirrored video, finger landmarks, timestamps, swipe paths, intended
words, and accepted/retry/skipped status. Practice/retry traces are not exported
as accepted training examples. Full code, model, and setup launchers are included.
